“Stronger” by Kanye West, featuring Daft Punk (they are the guys with helmets at the beginning). Clearly the video is based on [Akira](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akira_\(film\)), and the music samples Daft Punk’s [Harder, Better, Faster, Stronger](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harder,_Better,_Faster,_Stronger). Unfortunately the lyrics are typical contemporary rap junk. Oh well.
