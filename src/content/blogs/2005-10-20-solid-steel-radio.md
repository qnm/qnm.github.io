I've recently rediscovered the joy of radio, [Solid Steel Radio](http://www.ninjatune.net/solidsteel/) in particular. That show is tremendous!!

I've always enjoyed Coldcut, but their radio show is the best thing ever. Great music, and impeccably mixed. I downloaded a torrent of their September 2005 shows (available from your local friendly torrent retailer) and love the Strictly Kev mix - it meanders all over the place, but never loses the thread. Ace!
