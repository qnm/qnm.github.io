Last night, the [TSA](http://www.thesoundalliance.net) tech posse went on tour to [Webjam3](http://www.webjam.com.au), Sydney's premier 3-minute web presentation evening. Of course, we had a vested interest in the evening, as our very own [Aaron Wallis](http://this.isnot.tv) was presenting the [Samesame](http://www.samesame.com.au) [](http://this.isnot.tv)viral, [Yes, Prime Minister](http://www.samesame.com.au/yesprimeminister). His presentation was excellent, with some risque bullet points, LOLcats, and a cartoon John Howard saying pretty much anything you like! In second place was Webjam2 winner, [Mr. Speaker](http://mrspeaker.webeisteddfod.com/), but the real star of the evening was Mr. HTML5, [Lachlan Hunt](http://lachy.id.au), who provided us with an insight into the HTML5 video tag, followed by the [best Spice Girls lipsync and dance evah!](http://lachy.id.au/log/2007/06/webjam3) Being the nerds we are, we also caught up with [twitter](http://www.twitter.com) buddy, [Synapse](http://www.twitter.com/Synapse), and had a good yarn about many things, including a possible Webjam4 entry! Afterwards, in true TSA style, we then headed off to Yu to see Tiga and Bangang and grabbed a few beers whilst we were there.
