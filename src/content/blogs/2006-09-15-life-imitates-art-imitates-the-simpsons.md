[James Henstridge](http://blogs.gnome.org/view/jamesh/2006/09/14/0) noticed the similarity between [recent political debate](http://www.abc.net.au/news/newsitems/200609/s1741675.htm) on immigration and the '[Much Apu about nothing](http://www.snpp.com/episodes/3F20.html)' episode from The Simpsons.

> **Moe**: You know what really aggravazes me? It's them immigants. They wants all the benefits of living in Springfield, but they ain't even bother to learn themselves the language. **Homer**: Hey, those are exactly my sentimonies.

Substitute Moe for Howard, Homer for Beazley and Springfield for Australia, and you pretty much sum things up.
