The ever-relevant [Joel Spolsky](http://www.joelonsoftware.com) has a really good piece on [how to prioritise software development](http://www.joelonsoftware.com/articles/SetYourPriorities.html). Having worked for a company that implements every feature a customer wants (and felt the pain that comes with it) I can see where he's coming from in preferring the 'shrinkware' approach to features. One size can indeed fit all...
