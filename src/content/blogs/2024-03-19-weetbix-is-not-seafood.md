I really like the Shopping List feature in iOS Reminders, especially the Siri
ability to add items. It does a good job of classifying those items, too. Being
able to quickly shop from dept to dept guided by the list classification is
super handy.

That said, I think the classification is a little off sometimes. Maybe some
localisation is needed to prevent Weetbix being in the "Seafood" section. I am
curious what product it thinks Weetbix is though!
