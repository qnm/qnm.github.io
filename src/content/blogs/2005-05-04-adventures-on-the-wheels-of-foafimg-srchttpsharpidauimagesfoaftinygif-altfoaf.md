I've finally set up [FOAF](http://www.foaf-project.org/) on my site, using Leigh Dodds' [Foaf-a-matic](http://www.ldodds.com/foaf/foaf-a-matic.html) to create the base RDF. You can [explore my FOAF file](http://xml.mfd-consult.dk/foaf/explorer/?foaf=http%3A%2F%2Fsharp.id.au%2Ffoaf.rdf) if you want to. Now what am I going to do? Well hopefully someone reading this will also use FOAF, and they can link in to me. I was quite intrigued by [orkut](http://www.orkut.com) when it first came out, but it's been quite slow and a bit unreliable recently. I wish there was a way to turn my orkut friends list in to a FOAF file... watch this space.
