Whilst I thought I would never buy another Nokia product, the magic words 'Debian', 'Linux' and 'Open Development Platform' may yet change my mind. According to [Mobileburn](http://www.mobileburn.com/review.jsp?Id=1376) the device has an ARM based processor and is based on [GNOME!](http://gnome.org) [Havoc](http://ometer.com/) mentioned [Maemo](http://http://www.maemo.org/) on the desktop-devel-list and the vibe about the platform seems to be positive. Update: Looks like [Jeff has blogged about it too](http://www.gnome.org/~jdub/blog/projects/gnome/1117031356)
