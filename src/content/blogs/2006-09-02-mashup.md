Whilst doing some investigation into geomapping using [Google Maps](http://maps.google.com) [et al](http://www.zoomin.com.au), I stumbled upon [Mashup](http://blogs.smh.com.au/mashup/) on the [SMH](http://www.smh.com.au/) website. The blog on [Google Maps adding Sydney street data](http://blogs.smh.com.au/mashup/archives//004599.html) turned up an absolute gem - [bikely.com](http://www.bikely.com). For a while I've been wondering how to commute from my home in Cronulla to [my office](http://www.inthemix.com.au), and I've [finally found the answer](http://www.bikely.com/maps/bike-path/Cronulla-to-Central-Pitt-St). Hopefully I'll begin to shed some of my spare tyre if I do that a couple of times a week!
