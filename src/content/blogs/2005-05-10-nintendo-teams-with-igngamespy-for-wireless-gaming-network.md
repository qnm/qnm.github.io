"Nintendo has teamed up with IGN/Gamespy to offer a Wi-Fi gaming network to allow gamers to compete across the internet. This is vaguely interesting, except for the fact that they've announced their first game: Animal Crossing DS. The first Animal Crossing was a love-it-or-hate-it thing, but I'll give you one look inside my Animal Crossing lunchbox to determine my position. Being able to trade things wireless over the internet will finally give me a reason to turn on my DS again." [Check out the press release](http://press.nintendo.com/articles.jsp?id=6849).
