Quite a few blogs have noted an ongoing series in a [British Columbian newspaper](http://www.thetyee.ca/) about the 100-mile diet, where a couple are [attempting to live for a year on nothing but locally grown produce](http://www.thetyee.ca/Life/2005/06/28/HundredMileDiet/). It's an interesting insight into our growing dependence on imported food and the problems faced when trying to 'do the right thing' and eat locally.
