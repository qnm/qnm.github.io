After seeing [this article](http://gtd.marvelz.com/blog/2007/01/06/gtd-hack-for-pocket-moleskine-infobook/) on [this](http://gtd.marvelz.com/blog/) [GTD](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Getting_Things_Done) website, I've ordered myself a nice new [Moleskine Infobook](http://www.moleskineus.com/infobook.html) from [Pencity](http://www.pencity.com.au) to keep track of what I need to get done. In combination with the [pocketmod](http://www.pocketmod.com/), it's a deliberately low-tech alternative to using a PDA.
