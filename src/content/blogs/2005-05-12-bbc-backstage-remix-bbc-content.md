The BBC have always had an excellent website, and with [recent uses of technology](http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/technology/4447557.stm) have kept themseleves at the forefront of broadcasting tech. This continues with [BBC Backstage](http://backstage.bbc.co.uk/) which gives access to content and a bunch of APIs and invites you to play. [Google](http://google.com) have a similar approach, and people come up with all kinds of innovate hacks, such as the [Craigslist Google Map](http://paulrademacher.com/housing/). Can't wait to see what people do with this!
