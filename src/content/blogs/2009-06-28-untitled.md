from [tinkerlog](http://tinkerlog.com/2009/06/25/64-synchronizing-fireflies/ "tinkerlog") “Last week I invested some time to solder 64 [Firefly](http://tinkerlog.com/howto/synchronizing-firefly-how-to/) boards. Only 2.432 solder joints later I was ready for some videos.

Every firefly acts completely autonomously, it has its own tiny controller, eye and luminary. They are all connected for power supply only.”
