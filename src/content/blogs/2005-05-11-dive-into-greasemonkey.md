[Mark Pilgrim](http://diveintomark.org) has published his latest free online book, [Dive Into Greasmonkey](http://diveintogreasemonkey.org/). Greasemonkey is a javascript-based site modification tool, that alters your current view in whatever way you choose. A good exmple of this is the [del.icio.us integration with Radio 3's website](http://www.hackdiary.com/archives/000067.html) by [Matt Biddulph](http://hackdiary.com).
