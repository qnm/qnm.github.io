This morning my head hurts from a) beer last night and b) a mild allergy to mozzie bites. However, a) was most definitely worth it as I was at [Webjam](http://www.webjam.com.au)! [Earle Castledine](http://mrspeaker.webeisteddfod.com/) got first place with his Web Sequins demo, which will "make all your headings look like a Commodore 64 demo". It's a smart way of using CSS image maps to create monospaced fonts, by mapping text to image co-ordinates. His presentation was excellent too, so it was a worthy win. Flickr has a [bunch of pics](http://flickr.com/photos/charliebrewer/sets/72157594563531948/) from the night too. Now I've just gotta get through Friday to make it to geekfest part deux - [BarCamp](http://www.barcampsydney.org).
