[Zeldman](http://zeldman.com) pointed me in the direction of [this blog article](http://www.pixelcharmer.com/fieldnotes/archives/process_designing/2005/000622.html#000622), which describes a phenomenon in landscape architecture known as 'Lines of Desire', whereby 'users' take a shortcut across grass instead of using a walkway. This is analogous to web site maps, where the way a user wants to navigate somewhere may not be exactly the way the designer intended, [a bit like what happened here](http://www.peterme.com/archives/000073.html). But [it isn't always best letting the user decide.](http://atomiq.org/archives/2005/02/ceci_nest_pas_un_sentier.html)
